Questions

Is this timer free?

Yes. No signup or download.

How do I start?

Select Start. Pause or resume any time. Reset returns to 10:00; Undo restores your previous timer.

Will it work in the background?

The countdown catches up when you return. Sleep, a locked screen or browser suspension can delay the alert. Keep the page open and your device awake for an on-time alert.

How does the chime work?

Enable Chime and select Test sound. Playback depends on browser permissions and device volume. After a reload, test the sound again to re-enable it.

What is Focus view?

It hides the rest of the page. Press Escape or Exit focus view to return.

Does it save progress or block apps?

No account sync, session history, app blocking or Roman rewards. This tab restores your timer and task on reload when browser storage is available. Mac and iPhone app features differ.
