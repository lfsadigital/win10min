Looking for a Pomodoro timer for Mac?

Win10min for Mac is a native focus timer for Apple Silicon Macs. Use it on its own, or add the optional Win10min Planner in ChatGPT to turn a goal into a plan you can review. Completed sessions add to the Roman city saved on this Mac.

Install Win10min for Mac

  1. Check compatibility. Win 10 Minutes requires an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) and macOS 26 or later.
  2. Install from the App Store. Open Win 10 Minutes for Mac, install it and open it from Applications.
  3. Choose a small task. Coding, Writing and Studying start at ten minutes. Adjust the duration, name a concrete outcome and start focus.
  4. Review the work. A completed timer records focus time; record your result separately when using a plan.

Choosing a Pomodoro timer for Mac or a shorter focus session

A traditional Pomodoro routine alternates 25 minutes of work with a short break. If starting feels difficult, a ten-minute session gives you a smaller first commitment. Choose the duration around a concrete task: outline three points, review five notes or draft one paragraph.

Win10min is a focus timer with Roman progress rewards. Choose one task, set a clear stopping point and review what you completed when the session ends. For a shorter routine, follow the ten-minute focus guide.

Which timer fits your workflow?

If you want automatic 25-minute work and five-minute break cycles, check that your chosen Pomodoro app supports that exact routine. Win10min’s Mac app is presented here as a short-session alternative, not a promise of automatic Pomodoro cycles.

To try a shorter routine: choose one concrete action, focus for ten minutes, review the result, then take a break before deciding whether to continue. You can test the rhythm with the free ten-minute browser timer before downloading the Mac app.

App hiding and app blocking are different

Win10min for Mac can hide selected app windows to reduce visible distractions. You can reopen those apps. It does not lock applications or block websites. The iPhone app uses a separate Apple Screen Time blocking system.

If you need a Mac website blocker, the Mac app does not provide that feature. Keep the tools required for your task available and choose distraction controls that match your needs.

Use ChatGPT planning when you want it

  1. Open Win10min Planner by LFSA Digital in ChatGPT.
  2. Install the plugin and connect a Win10min account when prompted. Public installation currently opens account authorization.
  3. Describe your goal and review the proposed actions. Save the plan to your Win10min account when you want to use it later.
  4. In the Mac app, open GPT plan → Connect account and use the same Win10min account. Review a saved plan and start focus manually.

The Mac timer works without ChatGPT or a Win10min account. ChatGPT planning and the Mac timer are separate steps. Connecting an account does not start a timer, hide apps or sync Roman progress. See Planner support if setup fails.

Free timers and optional Mac Pro

Timers, presets, plans and recorded results are free. An optional Mac Pro subscription adds app-window hiding. Review current features and local prices in the app. iPhone and Mac subscriptions remain separate in the public release.

Watch the construction workshop

Open Focus → View Rome or the Empire page, then select Construction workshop. Workers approach the building site while a focus session is active, and the construction follows your session progress.

Your city still shows your earned buildings. The workshop is an illustrated scene: its background is decorative and its foreground building represents the current construction. Switching views does not change your saved rewards.

Where your progress lives

The Mac app stores its progress and settings on this Mac. It does not sync progress, purchases or settings with iPhone. Mac Arena, the iPhone campaign features and full iPhone analytics are not included in the Mac app.
