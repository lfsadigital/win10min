How to start a ten-minute focus session

A ten-minute focus session is a short block of time for one small task. Choose an outcome you can describe, keep the tools you need available and reduce distractions. When the timer ends, review your work before starting another session.

Make the task small enough to start

“Study biology” or “finish my presentation” describes a project. A focus session needs a smaller next action. Try “read two pages and write three notes,” “draft the opening slide” or “record one rehearsal.” You can keep the larger goal, but give this session a clear stopping point.

Ten minutes is a starting duration, not a promise that the whole task will be finished. If the work needs more time, decide what to do next after the session. If the step is too large, split it before you start.

A simple focus routine

  1. Name one outcome. Write down the exact page, paragraph or action you will work on. Avoid a list of unrelated tasks.
  2. Prepare your tools. Open the document, book or recording software you need. Put distracting tabs and notifications aside. Keep required apps available when selecting your distraction rules.
  3. Start the timer. Work on the chosen action for the session. If another idea appears, jot it down so you can return to it later.
  4. Review the result. At the end, check the work itself. A completed timer records focus time; it does not verify that your assignment or project is finished.
  5. Choose the next step. Take a break, repeat the task or move to another small action. Use what you learned to make the next session's goal more realistic.

Set up Win10min on your device

Win10min adds Roman progress to completed focus sessions. The iPhone app and Mac beta use different distraction controls. Choose the version for the device you will use; their settings, progress and purchases do not sync.

On iPhone or iPad

Install Win 10 Minutes: Focus Timer from the App Store. Open the app and follow its Screen Time authorization steps if you want app blocking. Choose your task, review the blocking settings available to your account and start the focus session. A timer alone does not establish that Screen Time permission has been granted.

The iPhone app includes Roman empire campaigns, focus statistics and Arena. It is free to download with in-app purchases. Review current local prices and premium features in the app before purchasing.

On Mac

Download the Mac beta from the homepage, unzip it and drag Win10min Preview.app into Applications. Open that app, choose a task and review the apps you want hidden during the session. Keep any tools required for the task available, then start.

The Mac beta requires Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 26 or later. It hides app windows rather than applying iPhone-style Screen Time shields. It does not block websites or lock applications. The timer and saved Roman progress work without ChatGPT.

Can ChatGPT plan a session?

The Mac beta has an optional local planning connection. In the app's GPT page, choose Install connection, then select Win10min for Mac in a new compatible local Work/Codex chat. Ask for a small plan and explicitly ask to send it to Win10min when you want it queued. Start the plan yourself in the Mac app.

This connection is not available in ChatGPT web or mobile. Planning uses the connected account's allowance and applicable credits. If an unfinished plan is already saved, finish or dismiss that plan before sending a replacement. Opening the Mac app and replacing a plan are separate actions.

Is a ten-minute session the Pomodoro Technique?

A short focus timer is not the full Pomodoro Technique. Francesco Cirillo's official explanation describes a wider system that includes planning, interruption management and effort estimation. Win10min's short sessions offer a different starting routine with Roman rewards; using a timer does not make it an official Pomodoro product.

If the setup does not work

On Mac, look in Applications for Win10min Preview, which is the beta's actual app name. On iPhone, review Screen Time authorization and the app's blocking settings. If you still need help, send your device, system version and the exact issue to Win10min support. Do not include passwords or private task content.
